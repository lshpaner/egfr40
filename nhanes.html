NHANES Example

The repository includes a pipeline that applies the equation to the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) 2017–March 2020 pre-pandemic cycle. It lives in the repository, not the installed package.

git clone https://github.com/lshpaner/egfr40.git
cd egfr40
pip install -e ".[nhanes]"
make data_gen     # fetch the raw NHANES files into data/raw/
make preprocess   # merge, compute eGFR, recode -> data/processed/

notebooks/01_score_nhanes.ipynb then:

  1. scores every participant (one egfr40_3year value per SEQN)

  2. shows per-participant scores, including the highest-risk participants

  3. produces survey-weighted risk summaries by stratum

  4. picks real participants whose inputs sit closest to the online calculator’s dropdown values and lists exactly what to enter on ckdpcrisk.org/gfrdecline40, so the package can be checked against the calculator on real data

How NHANES variables are coded

Input

NHANES source and coding

eGFR

CKD-EPI 2021 from serum creatinine (LBXSCR)

uACR

URDACT, mg/g

Systolic BP

mean of up to three oscillometric readings (BPXOSY1-3)

Antihypertensive medication

BPQ050A (now taking), falling back to BPQ040A; people never told they have hypertension are coded 0

Heart failure

self-reported (MCQ160B)

CHD

self-reported coronary heart disease (MCQ160C) or heart attack (MCQ160E)

Atrial fibrillation

not collected in NHANES; set to 0

Smoking

fewer than 100 lifetime cigarettes = never; smoking now (every day or some days) = current; quit = former

Diabetes

self-reported diagnosis, insulin or diabetes pills, or HbA1c ≥6.5%

The analysis includes adults aged 20 to 80 who are not pregnant, have eGFR ≥15 and have complete model inputs.

Prior work

The 3-year risk distribution in U.S. adults has already been described with NHANES 1999–2020 in Individualized Risk of CKD Progression among US Adults (Journal of the American Society of Nephrology, doi:10.1681/ASN.0000000000000377). The egfr40 pipeline reproduces that kind of analysis and is intended as a worked, reproducible example.
