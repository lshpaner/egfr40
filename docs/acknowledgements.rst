Acknowledgements
================

The risk equation developed by Grams et al. for the CKD Prognosis Consortium
offers a validated way to identify people at risk of kidney function decline
before it becomes advanced, including people without diagnosed chronic kidney
disease.

``egfr40`` is based on the models and conversion equations in the studies
listed in :doc:`references`. Please refer to them for a full understanding of
how the models were developed and validated.

``egfr40`` is an independent implementation and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by the CKD Prognosis Consortium.
