Common pitfalls

uACR in mg/mmol. The model expects mg/g. Values reported in mg/mmol are about 8.84 times smaller, which understates risk. Convert first with acr_mg_mmol_to_mg_g.

HbA1c in mmol/mol. The model expects percent. The paper’s tables label HbA1c “mmol”, but the centering at 7 in Supplemental Table S5 shows the unit is percent. Convert IFCC values with % = mmol/mol / 10.929 + 2.15.

eGFR from an older equation. The model was built on CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free) eGFR. eGFR from the 2009 equation or MDRD differs, especially near the eGFR 60 boundary. Recompute with ckd_epi_2021 when serum creatinine is available.

The eGFR 60 boundary. The two eGFR strata are separate models and do not meet at 60. Small changes in eGFR around 60 can move risk noticeably. At exactly 60 the two models are averaged, as on the CKD-PC website.

Inputs the model does not use. Race and hypertension status do not appear in the published equation, so neither is an input here.

Missing values in cohorts. add_egfr40_risk_col returns NaN for rows with a missing required input. Count them before summarizing risk, since complete-case results may not represent the full cohort.
