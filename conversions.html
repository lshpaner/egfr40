uPCR and Dipstick to uACR

The model needs uACR in mg/g. When only a urine protein-to-creatinine ratio (uPCR) or a dipstick reading is available, egfr40 can estimate uACR with the crude (unadjusted) equations of Sumida et al. (2020). Grams et al. (2022) used the same conversions when developing the model.

Warning

Converted values are approximations that carry measurement error. A directly measured uACR is preferred when available.

uPCR to uACR

from egfr40 import pcr_to_acr

pcr_to_acr(150)          # uPCR 150 mg/g -> about 32.8 mg/g
pcr_to_acr([50, 500])    # arrays work too
\[\begin{split}\begin{aligned} \text{uACR} = \exp\Big(\, & 5.3920 + 0.3072 \log\left(\min\left(\tfrac{\text{PCR}}{50},\, 1\right)\right) \\ & + 1.5793 \log\left(\max\left(\min\left(\tfrac{\text{PCR}}{500},\, 1\right),\, 0.1\right)\right) \\ & + 1.1266 \log\left(\max\left(\tfrac{\text{PCR}}{500},\, 1\right)\right) \Big) \end{aligned}\end{split}\]

with uPCR and uACR in mg/g and natural logarithms. Non-positive or missing uPCR returns NaN.

Note

Why this differs from kfre. Sumida et al. (2020) publish two versions of this equation: a crude one (uPCR only) and an adjusted one that adds sex, diabetes and hypertension. egfr40 uses the crude equation because Grams et al. (2022) converted uPCR with the unadjusted equation when developing the model. The kfre library uses the adjusted equation, so the same uPCR can give uACR values that differ by up to about 20% between the two libraries, depending on the individual.

Dipstick to uACR

from egfr40 import dipstick_to_acr

dipstick_to_acr("++")    # about 337 mg/g

Unit conversion

from egfr40 import acr_mg_mmol_to_mg_g, acr_mg_g_to_mg_mmol

acr_mg_mmol_to_mg_g(3.0)   # 26.5 mg/g
acr_mg_g_to_mg_mmol(30)    # 3.4 mg/mmol

1 mg/mmol equals 8.84 mg/g.
