The Model

Outcome

The model predicts a composite of a ≥40% decline in eGFR or kidney failure (receipt of kidney replacement therapy) over 2 to 3 years. A 40% eGFR decline is accepted as a surrogate kidney endpoint by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the European Medicines Agency.

Development

Grams et al. (2022) developed the model in an individual participant data meta-analysis of 1,621,817 participants from 43 cohorts in the CKD Prognosis Consortium. A logistic regression was fit in each development cohort and the estimates were pooled by random-effects meta-analysis. Separate models were fit within four strata: diabetes or not, and eGFR ≥60 or <60 mL/min/1.73 m².

egfr40 implements model 2 from the paper, the version used by the online calculator. Model 3 adds the prior 2-year eGFR slope and improved discrimination only slightly, so it is not included.

The equation

For each stratum,

\[p = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-\left(\beta_0 + \sum_i \beta_i x_i\right)}}\]

where \(\beta_0\) is the stratum’s intercept and each \(x_i\) is a predictor after the centering and scaling shown below.

E = 85 in the eGFR ≥60 strata and 45 in the eGFR <60 strata.

Every coefficient matches the odds ratios reported in the paper’s Tables 2 and 3 (exp(β) agrees to two decimals), and the package reproduces the online calculator in the diabetes strata (eGFR <60 and ≥60).

The eGFR 60 boundary

Because the strata are separate models, predicted risk jumps at eGFR 60. For a 60-year-old man with uACR 10 mg/g, SBP 130 and no other risk factors, the risk is 0.64% at eGFR 61 and 1.89% at eGFR 59. Following the CKD-PC website, risk at exactly eGFR 60 is the average of the two models.

What is not implemented

  • Hypertension-based models (Supplemental Tables S6 and S7) are published without intercepts, so absolute risk cannot be computed.

  • 30% and 50% decline models (Supplemental Tables S8 to S11) are also published without intercepts.

  • Race and hypertension status do not appear in the published equation.

  • Risk thresholds such as “high risk” are not defined in the paper, so the package reports probabilities only.

Discrimination

Median C statistics of model 2 in the validation cohorts were 0.740 (eGFR ≥60, no diabetes), 0.774 (eGFR ≥60, diabetes), 0.750 (eGFR <60, no diabetes) and 0.769 (eGFR <60, diabetes).
