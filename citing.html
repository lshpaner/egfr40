Citing egfr40

If you use egfr40 in your research or projects, please cite it along with the original paper by Grams et al. (2022).

@software{shpaner_2026_24350438,
  author       = {Shpaner, Leonid},
  title        = {{egfr40: A Python Library for the CKD Prognosis
                   Consortium 40\% eGFR Decline Risk Equation}},
  month        = nov,
  year         = 2026,
  publisher    = {Zenodo},
  doi          = {10.5281/zenodo.24350438},
  url          = {https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24350438}
}

The DOI above always resolves to the latest version. Each release also has its own version DOI, listed on the Zenodo record.
