API Reference

Risk

egfr40.egfr40_risk(age, male, egfr, uacr, sbp, bp_meds, hf, chd, afib, smoking, bmi, dm, hba1c=None, oral_dm=0, insulin=0, explain=False)[source]

3-year probability of a >=40% eGFR decline or kidney failure.

Parameters:

  • age (float) – Age in years.

  • male (int) – 1 = male, 0 = female.

  • egfr (float) – eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m^2, from the CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation.

  • uacr (float) – Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio in mg/g. Must be greater than 0.

  • sbp (float) – Systolic blood pressure in mmHg.

  • bp_meds (int) – Antihypertensive medication use (1/0).

  • hf (int) – History of heart failure (1/0).

  • chd (int) – History of coronary heart disease (1/0).

  • afib (int) – History of atrial fibrillation (1/0).

  • smoking ({'never', 'former', 'current'}) – Smoking status.

  • bmi (float) – Body mass index in kg/m^2.

  • dm (bool) – Diabetes. Required: selects the diabetes or no-diabetes model.

  • hba1c (float, optional) – HbA1c in percent. Required when dm is True.

  • oral_dm (int, default 0) – Oral diabetes medication (1/0). Used only when dm is True.

  • insulin (int, default 0) – Insulin use (1/0). Used only when dm is True.

  • explain (bool, default False) – Return a breakdown instead of just the risk.

Returns:

The 3-year probability, between 0 and 1. At eGFR == 60 the two eGFR strata are averaged, as on ckdpcrisk.org.

With explain=True, a dict with risk, stratum, intercept, linear_predictor (log-odds) and contributions (each predictor’s log-odds contribution, largest first). Positive contributions raise risk relative to the reference individual: age 60, eGFR at the stratum center (85 or 45), uACR 10 mg/g, SBP 130, BMI 30, HbA1c 7%, never smoker, no BP or diabetes medication, and HF and CHD at their centering values of 0.05 and 0.15 (so an individual without HF or CHD gets a small negative contribution for each). At eGFR == 60, a dict with the averaged risk and both breakdowns under strata.

Return type:

float or dict

Raises:

ValueError – If hba1c is missing for an individual with diabetes, uacr is not positive, or smoking is not a recognized value.

Examples

>>> egfr40_risk(age=70, male=1, egfr=40, uacr=150, sbp=140, bp_meds=1,
...             hf=0, chd=1, afib=1, smoking="former", bmi=28,
...             dm=True, hba1c=7)
0.166...
egfr40.add_egfr40_risk_col(df, age_col='age', sex_col='sex', egfr_col='eGFR', uacr_col='uACR', sbp_col='sbp', bp_meds_col='bp_meds', hf_col='hf', chd_col='chd', afib_col='afib', smoking_col='smoking', bmi_col='bmi', dm_col='dm', hba1c_col='hba1c', oral_dm_col='oral_dm', insulin_col='insulin', risk_col='egfr40_3year', copy=True)[source]

Add the 3-year >=40% eGFR decline risk to every row of a DataFrame.

Each row is scored with its own stratum (eGFR >=60 or <60, with or without diabetes). Rows at exactly eGFR 60 get the average of the two eGFR strata, as on ckdpcrisk.org.

Parameters:

  • df (pandas.DataFrame) – One row per individual.

  • age_col (str) – Column names for age (years), sex, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), uACR (mg/g), systolic BP (mmHg) and antihypertensive medication use.

  • sex_col (str) – Column names for age (years), sex, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), uACR (mg/g), systolic BP (mmHg) and antihypertensive medication use.

  • egfr_col (str) – Column names for age (years), sex, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), uACR (mg/g), systolic BP (mmHg) and antihypertensive medication use.

  • uacr_col (str) – Column names for age (years), sex, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), uACR (mg/g), systolic BP (mmHg) and antihypertensive medication use.

  • sbp_col (str) – Column names for age (years), sex, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), uACR (mg/g), systolic BP (mmHg) and antihypertensive medication use.

  • bp_meds_col (str) – Column names for age (years), sex, eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021), uACR (mg/g), systolic BP (mmHg) and antihypertensive medication use.

  • hf_col (str) – Column names for history of heart failure, coronary heart disease and atrial fibrillation.

  • chd_col (str) – Column names for history of heart failure, coronary heart disease and atrial fibrillation.

  • afib_col (str) – Column names for history of heart failure, coronary heart disease and atrial fibrillation.

  • smoking_col (str) – Column names for smoking status and BMI (kg/m^2).

  • bmi_col (str) – Column names for smoking status and BMI (kg/m^2).

  • dm_col (str) – Column names for diabetes, HbA1c (%), oral diabetes medication and insulin. The last three are used only for rows with diabetes and may be absent from df if no row has diabetes.

  • hba1c_col (str) – Column names for diabetes, HbA1c (%), oral diabetes medication and insulin. The last three are used only for rows with diabetes and may be absent from df if no row has diabetes.

  • oral_dm_col (str) – Column names for diabetes, HbA1c (%), oral diabetes medication and insulin. The last three are used only for rows with diabetes and may be absent from df if no row has diabetes.

  • insulin_col (str) – Column names for diabetes, HbA1c (%), oral diabetes medication and insulin. The last three are used only for rows with diabetes and may be absent from df if no row has diabetes.

  • risk_col (str, default "egfr40_3year") – Name of the new risk column.

  • copy (bool, default True) – Work on a copy and leave df unchanged.

Returns:

df (or a copy) with risk_col added, holding probabilities between 0 and 1.

Return type:

pandas.DataFrame

Notes

Sex accepts "male"/"female", "m"/"f" or 1/0. Yes/no columns (BP medication, HF, CHD, AFib, diabetes, oral diabetes medication, insulin) accept 1/0 or "yes"/"no". Smoking accepts "never", "former" or "current".

A row with any required input missing gets NaN. HbA1c, oral diabetes medication and insulin are required only for rows with diabetes; missing values there give NaN rather than being treated as 0.

Raises:

ValueError – If a smoking or yes/no column contains an unrecognized value.

egfr40.ckd_epi_2021(scr, age, male)[source]

eGFR from serum creatinine, using the CKD-EPI 2021 (race-free) equation.

Inker LA et al. N Engl J Med 2021;385:1737-1749.

Parameters:

  • scr (float or array-like) – Serum creatinine in mg/dL.

  • age (float or array-like) – Age in years.

  • male (int or array-like) – 1 = male, 0 = female.

Returns:

eGFR in mL/min/1.73 m^2. NaN where inputs are missing.

Return type:

float or numpy.ndarray

Examples

>>> round(ckd_epi_2021(scr=1.0, age=50, male=1), 1)
91.7

Conversions

egfr40.pcr_to_acr(pcr)[source]

Estimate uACR from the urine protein-to-creatinine ratio (uPCR).

Uses the crude equation of Sumida et al. (2020): exp(5.3920 + 0.3072 log(min(PCR/50, 1)) + 1.5793 log(max(min(PCR/500, 1), 0.1)) + 1.1266 log(max(PCR/500, 1))).

Parameters:

pcr (float or array-like) – uPCR in mg/g.

Returns:

Estimated uACR in mg/g. NaN for missing or non-positive uPCR.

Return type:

float or numpy.ndarray

Examples

>>> round(pcr_to_acr(150), 1)
32.8
egfr40.dipstick_to_acr(category)[source]

Estimate uACR from a urine dipstick protein category.

Uses the crude equation of Sumida et al. (2020).

Parameters:

category (str or list of str) – "negative", "trace", "+", "++" or ">++". Also accepts "neg", "1+", "2+", "3+", "4+" and "+++".

Returns:

Estimated uACR in mg/g. NaN for missing values.

Return type:

float or numpy.ndarray

Raises:

ValueError – If a category is not recognized.

Examples

>>> round(dipstick_to_acr("++"), 1)
