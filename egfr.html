eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021)

The model was developed using eGFR from the race-free CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine equation (Inker et al., 2021). ckd_epi_2021 computes it from serum creatinine, age and sex.

from egfr40 import ckd_epi_2021

ckd_epi_2021(scr=1.2, age=70, male=1)   # mL/min/1.73 m²

It accepts scalars or arrays and returns NaN where inputs are missing.

The equation

\[\begin{split}\begin{aligned} \text{eGFR} = 142 \, & \times \min\left(\frac{S_{cr}}{\kappa},\, 1\right)^{\alpha} \times \max\left(\frac{S_{cr}}{\kappa},\, 1\right)^{-1.200} \\ & \times 0.9938^{\text{age}} \times 1.012 \; [\text{if female}] \end{aligned}\end{split}\]

where \(S_{cr}\) is serum creatinine in mg/dL, \(\kappa\) is 0.7 for females and 0.9 for males, and \(\alpha\) is -0.241 for females and -0.302 for males.

Note

The supplement of Grams et al. (2022) prints the female constant as 143, which is 142 × 1.012 rounded. egfr40 uses the exact published equation, so hand calculations from that table can differ slightly.
